Eigenvalues & eigenvectors of M
        

        
M v = λ v. Real eigenvectors keep their line under M; negative λ reverses direction and zero λ collapses it. A complex conjugate pair spans a real invariant plane, drawn as an ellipse using the real and imaginary parts of its vector. These describe the fixed input M during animation. General eigenvectors need not be orthogonal or independent; defective matrices may repeat a direction.

      

      
Purple arrows: real eigenvectors of M. Gold ellipse: complex invariant plane.
