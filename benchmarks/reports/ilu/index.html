    Final solve summary & numerical method
    Both solvers start at zero and stop at a true residual of at most 10⁻⁸ ‖b‖₂; a limit or breakdown is shown explicitly. Residuals need not decrease at every iteration. Colors outside 0–100 are clipped.
    Diffusion alone produces a symmetric positive-definite five-point system. First-order upwind flow makes it nonsymmetric, so that mode uses GMRES. Jacobi scales by the diagonal; ILU(0) performs incomplete elimination within the stored pattern and applies two triangular solves. Every accepted solver step checks the true residual with a fresh sparse matrix-vector product. Saved solver timings include these convergence checks but exclude frame capture. The live animation captures actual accepted iterates.
