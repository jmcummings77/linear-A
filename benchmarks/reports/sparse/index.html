What is being calculated?

A five-point finite-difference stencil with positive face conductivities gives a symmetric positive-definite system. CSR stores its nonzero coefficients and row boundaries. Jacobi scales by the diagonal. Each step evaluates the true residual directly, so this implementation uses two sparse matrix-vector products per iteration.
