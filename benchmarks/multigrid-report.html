Method & provenance
A fixed symmetric preconditioner
Two weighted-Jacobi sweeps before and after coarse correction, with weight 2/3. Bilinear interpolation P and transpose restriction Pᵀ. Every level rediscretizes the unscaled five-point Dirichlet Laplacian; the coarse operator is not PᵀAP. The one-point solve is exact. This first implementation supports constant coefficients on nested square grids, not arbitrary sparse matrices or variable conductivity.
