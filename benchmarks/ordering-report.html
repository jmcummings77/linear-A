What exactly is being reordered?

With p[new] = old, the new system is B[i,j] = A[p[i],p[j]] and c[i] = b[p[i]]. After solving By = c, restore x[p[i]] = y[i]. The graph is the union of the stored pattern and its transpose, excluding diagonal entries. BFS starts at the unvisited vertex of smallest degree; degree ties use original indices. Reverse the complete traversal, including disconnected components. There is no pseudo-peripheral search. Structural bandwidth is max |row − column| over stored entries, or zero for an empty pattern.

The live system models steady transport and diffusion with a hot top boundary. The live shuffle uses a fixed seed; its generator differs from the recorded benchmark shuffle. Jacobi provides an ordering-neutral reference: permutation preserves the preconditioned system up to a change of coordinates, so residual curves should agree up to roundoff. Narrower bandwidth is a layout heuristic, not a guarantee of improved ILU convergence or total runtime. See the Cuthill–McKee algorithm description.
