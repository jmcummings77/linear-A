How to read these reports

Results are saved measurements from the recorded machine and workload. Changing a chart filter does not run a new benchmark.

Interactive demos run WebAssembly on your device. They explain the numerical behavior and are separate from the saved results.

Method & data explains the workload, verification, sample variation, and limitations. Use it before drawing conclusions across implementations or runs.

Historical source limitation. Runs marked as having working changes do not include the exact measured source. Their commit and source hash cannot reconstruct those changes. New published measurements require a clean source capture; historical timings remain available with this limitation.
