    What these snapshots can establish
    The range envelope [candidate min ÷ baseline max, candidate max ÷ baseline min] describes observed ranges; it is not a confidence interval. Medians and MAD are recomputed from the recorded samples; MAD is median absolute deviation. No significance test or performance gate is applied.
    Ratios are withheld for different seeds, numerical/layout contracts, timing boundaries, warmup rules, or explicit workload versions. Toolchain, build-command and hardware differences remain visible as confounding factors. Rows join only on the exact implementation, operation and size; a missing measurement is never zero.
    Samples are recorded batches. New reports identify each batch as a fresh process; historical reports may leave that unspecified. Processes on one host do not provide independent machines or eliminate thermal and scheduling effects. Small sample counts give weak evidence, and inspecting many rows increases the chance of noticing noise.
    Allocation is included in the existing timing contract, but these snapshots do not record allocation counts. No allocation delta is inferred. The separate locality study’s allocation data uses a different schema and cannot be mixed into this comparison.
